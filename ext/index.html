Usage Overlay extensions

Three small, unofficial browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox. Each one shows how much of your AI plan’s usage limits you have used, directly in the toolbar badge, a popup and an overlay on the service’s own website.

These extensions are not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google or Anthropic.

02How they work

  • Each extension uses the session you already have in your browser. You never enter a password or API key, and the extensions do not read, store or transmit your cookies or credentials.
  • They only keep the usage information the service itself shows you. Anything else in the responses, such as account details, is discarded immediately and never stored. Your conversations, prompts, code and other page content are never read.
  • The latest figures and your settings are stored locally in your browser’s extension storage and removed when you uninstall the extension.
  • There are no servers of our own. The only network requests go to the service the extension is made for.

The full details for each extension, including permissions, are in its privacy policy above.
