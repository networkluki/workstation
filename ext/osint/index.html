Privacy Policy

Forensic OSINT

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Forensic OSINT (“the extension”) is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that analyzes the web page in the current tab for risk indicators, collects information from it and takes screenshots, for your own open-source intelligence (OSINT) work.

01What the extension accesses

The extension accesses a page only when you click “Analyze this page” in its toolbar popup, and then only the tab that was active when you opened the popup. It has no access to any other page. From that tab it reads:

  • the page address, title, language, metadata (such as description, author and generator) and the canonical address;
  • the addresses of links, scripts, stylesheets, frames and form targets, the structure of forms (target address, method and field types) and the text of links;
  • email addresses and phone numbers that appear in the page’s links or text;
  • the names of cookies that the page’s own scripts can read (never cookie values);
  • a screenshot of the visible part of the tab and, if you choose full-page capture, a screenshot of the whole page.

It never reads what is typed into form fields, passwords, cookie values or browser history. It does not track your browsing and does nothing in the background.

Screenshots contain whatever the tab shows, which can include personal data, for example your own account details on a page you are signed in to.

02How the data is used

Only to build the report that you see: risk indicators, the collected information, the screenshots with their SHA-256 hashes, and pivot links.

03Where the data is stored

Reports are stored locally in your browser’s extension storage (IndexedDB) in the current browser profile. Besides the information above, each report records when it was made, the extension version, your browser’s user agent string and time zone (to document how and when the evidence was captured), and any note you write. The 10 newest reports are kept and older ones are deleted automatically. You can delete a report at any time from the report page, and all reports are removed when you uninstall the extension. The extension also stores your full-page capture setting locally.

When you export a report (HTML, JSON or PNG), the file is saved where you choose. What happens to exported files is up to you.

04Sharing

The extension sends no data to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising, uses no servers of its own and makes no network requests.

The report page contains pivot links to third-party services (Internet Archive Wayback Machine, crt.sh, rdap.org, Google Public DNS, VirusTotal, urlscan.io and the Google Transparency Report). Nothing is sent to them unless you click one of these links. The link then opens that service in a new tab and passes it the analyzed page’s domain, IP address or address. That service’s own privacy policy then applies.
