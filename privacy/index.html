Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

At ipconfig.se, your privacy is a core principle. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal data from visitors. Our service is designed to work without requiring any user information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information, usage data, analytics identifiers, cookies for tracking, or IP logs. Any IP address displayed on the site is processed temporarily in real time and is never stored on our servers.

How the Service Works

When you access ipconfig.se, your public IP address is detected only to display it back to you. This data is handled in memory for the duration of the request and is immediately discarded. No databases, logs, or third-party tracking systems are used.

Cookies and Tracking

ipconfig.se does not use tracking cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels, or fingerprinting technologies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit any user data to third parties — because no user data is collected.

Security

All connections are encrypted using HTTPS to protect data in transit. Since no personal information is stored, the risk of data exposure is effectively eliminated.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy or how the service works, you may contact us through the website or kund.theinfo@gmail.com.
